Admission Guidelines & Checklist

Guidelines & Regulations

  1. The College follows strict Islamic principles and values in discipline and student training.
  2. Shortlisted applicants sit entrance exams in two parts: Arabic/Islamic Subjects and English/Western Subjects.
  3. All fees must be paid at least two weeks prior to term commencement.
  4. Students may not leave campus grounds except on approved medical grounds via the Principal.

Document Checklist

  • Duly completed Application Form
  • Medical Doctor's Health Report
  • Reports from two (2) Referees — at least one a recognized Islamic scholar
  • Copy of Applicant's Birth Certificate
  • Four (4) recent passport-sized photographs
  • Application Processing Fee (₦15,000.00) — paid by bank transfer/deposit
  • Primary School Completion Certificate (if available)
